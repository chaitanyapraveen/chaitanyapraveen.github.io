Numerize

Co-Founder and CTO · earlier called Firstcourse Accounting
The problem

Most restaurants have their numbers spread across POS systems, bank accounts and paper invoices, and can't afford an accountant to pull it together.

What I did

We built the financial base for a restaurant's books. All the data goes into one reconciled ledger, and the reports are calculated from it: net payables and receivables, net profit, month-on-month growth, runway and price sensitivity.

Invoices are read by a multistage OCR pipeline, and a person checks the numbers before they go in. Cash entries are reconciled against the Chart of Accounts. A graph database tracks how transactions and financial entities are connected. I led a team of four engineers and mentored them on quality and testing.

Ocean engineering · 2012 to 2019

Sea6 Energy

Ocean Engineer, then Principal Engineer, Ocean Structures
The problem

Growing seaweed in the open sea needs floating structures that can take the waves without using too much material or too many anchors.

What I did

I joined as an ocean engineer, writing programs to simulate how floating structures and their moorings behave. Then I wrote a scientific program to estimate the loads on a structure across different wave conditions.

That work led me to design Dweep, a modular floating structure based on tensegrity, an idea I took from biological structures. The load is shared across the whole frame, so it needs less material and fewer anchors. I'm the first-named inventor on the US patent.
